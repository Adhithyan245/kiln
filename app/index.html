How to read an evidence trail

  1. Inspect a recorded pairChoose an item and the condition (`C1`–`C4`) and seed that produced its assessment.
  2. Play the verifier trailEach segment is one artifact. Accepted evidence heats up; rejected evidence turns striped grey and drops from the verdict; inconclusive evidence stays visibly grey.
  3. Review the proposalRead the cause, suggested fix, cost, thermochemical gauges, sources and limits. Review flags are prompts for a human, not measured outcomes.

The comparison

C1 runs a single agent, C2 isolated specialists, C3 a central planner and C4 a coordinating collective. The experiment compares their assessments and compute use at a locked budget.

Recorded, not running live

Playing a trail replays cached records. It never invokes a language model, starts a GPU calculation, or changes the scientific results.
